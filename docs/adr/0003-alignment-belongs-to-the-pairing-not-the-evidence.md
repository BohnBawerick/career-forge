# Alignment belongs to the pairing, not the Evidence

Evidence carries no status field. There is no unconfirmed, verified, approved or archived state. If a
record is in the Bank it is the person's own claim, whether they wrote it in a resume, typed it, or
said it in an Interview. Nothing is ever archived; the Bank only grows.

What varies is **Alignment**: how well one Evidence record answers one Requirement, held on the
pairing as strong, partial or none. The obvious design is a status on the Evidence, so the reason for
rejecting it needs recording. A status asks "do we believe this yet", which is the wrong question,
because the app has no way to answer it and no business doubting the person. Alignment asks "does
this answer what the Job wants", which is answerable and is what the product actually needs.

## Consequences

Alignment drives the whole Interview with no extra machinery:

- **strong** — say nothing, and do not offer a chance to wreck a good answer
- **partial** — ask for more, and accept "no, that's all" as a complete answer
- **none** — probe for a Gap, and report it honestly when there is nothing there
